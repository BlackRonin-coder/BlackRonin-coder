# Public Evidence Register

**Updated:** 8 October 2026

This register prevents a public explanation, synthetic test or private engineering claim from silently becoming evidence of commercial deployment or independently validated safety.

| Public resource | Evidence class | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Profile](../README.md) | Personal professional presentation | Career background and independent research focus | Third-party certification of AI controls |
| [NEOM Bindu case study](NEOM_Bindu_Public_Case_Study_2026.md) | Conditional scenario analysis | A modelled investment-sequencing alternative under stated assumptions | Actual NEOM earnings, adopted policy, commercial bankability |
| [CBAC public research note](CBAC_Public_Research_Note_2026.md) | Falsifiable research hypothesis | Scope, problem, proposed failure criteria and evaluation plan | Tested frontier-model containment |
| [Synthetic control code](../examples/cbac_synthetic_demo/README.md) | Public in-memory teaching demonstration | Its limited, test-defined behaviour in a mock environment | Authentic identity, authenticated provenance, complete mediation, private-system conformance, production safety |

## Planned work not yet demonstrated by public artefacts

- Model/agent testing under realistic adversarial conditions.
- Independent implementations with conformance and replacement evidence.
- Foreign-host complete-mediation and bypass evaluation.
- Independent external technical reproduction.

**Do not label these PASS** until independently inspectable evidence establishes the specific scoped claim.

## Public-release rules

1. Public documents must distinguish *designed*, *implemented*, *internally tested*, *independently reproduced* and *deployed*.
2. No private implementations, protected contracts, raw security logs, keys, credentials or confidential research materials are to be copied to the public repository.
3. No model's own success claim becomes accepted external evidence merely because it appears in a README.
4. A failed evaluation narrows the applicable claim; it is not a reason to conceal the result.
5. Preserve traceability from public claims to dated, independently reviewable evidence when it exists.
